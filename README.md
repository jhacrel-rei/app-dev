# My Favorite Series: Harry Potter

**Harry Potter** is a fantasy series about a boy who discovers on his 11th birthday that he is a *wizard*. It was written by J.K. Rowling and was my gateway into reading and movies. I enjoyed every bit of the series and would waste all of my time finishing everything all at once. I was also fond of their **accent**.

## Why I Love It

> It made me believe a that magic spells do exists... as they say.

I love it because of:

- The **magical world** (Hogwarts, Diagon Alley, Quidditch)
- The *friendship* of Harry, Ron, and Hermione
- The mystery that builds across all seven books
